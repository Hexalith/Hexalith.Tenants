# Independent divergence review

**One high and three medium findings.** The command safety rules need four clarifications for independently built surfaces. This review compared the working-tree architecture at SHA-256 `2e218ac1fd0e96522ffd084bce9bfbff8440161616739f088121260e34fc5042` with the PRD, implementation and regression tests.

| High | **Using stale evidence for a write.** One surface could reuse a valid stale 304 for display and treat the successful validation as sufficient to open a destructive preview.
