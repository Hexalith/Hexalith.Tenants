The November conformance table assigns separate owners and completion evidence for hosted qualification, the first enforcing consumer, deployed identity and index capacity. The four checks remain open.

**Final verdict: pass.** The revised route inventory and D2 use the message ID for status lookup and preserve correlation as verification evidence. AD-12 requires a fresh, ordered projection of the same aggregate to reach the verified command sequence and show its effect. AD-2 and D9 distinguish ordinary URL cursors from circuit-held search continuations. AD-15 specifies optimistic concurrency, contiguous progress and durable acknowledgement. All four documentation findings are resolved.
