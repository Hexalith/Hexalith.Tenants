## Intent

**Problem:** Ten open deferred-work items span five isolated areas: (1) `TenantConfigurationPrincipalResolver` swallows fatal exceptions into `Indeterminate` and drops late faults on canceled `GetAuthenticationStateAsync` tasks (`DW-716`); (2) `ValidateCanonicalBoundaryPackageCohort` lacks a documented, reconciled policy for `.0`/`+metadata` SemVer aliases (`DW-681`) and `scripts/verify-catalog-package-consumer.py` handles local coordinated candidate identities case-sensitively (`DW-688`); (3) `.github/workflows/recover-partial-release.yml` omits `references/Hexalith.Platform` (`DW-687`) and `scripts/verify-release-artifacts.sh` lets slow signature verification starve later packages' zero-poll immediate probes (`DW-673`); (4) `RefreshTenantAccess` lacks a module-owned McpCli conformance vector (`DW-692`) and `docs/development-route-catalog.md` / `TenantsDevelopmentRoutingCatalog` drift from `TENANTS_SOURCE_CATALOG_PROFILE` opt-in (`DW-682`); and (5) the browser evidence harness can truncate checkpoints (`DW-701`), hang failure diagnostics on a wedged renderer (`DW-702`), and mint unbounded live corpus read JWTs (`DW-704`).

**Approach:** Implement the five batches in parallel via dedicated subagents, verify with the full solution build, test projects, Python/Node guards, and McpCli vector validator, and close all ten items in `_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/deferred-work.md`.

**Approved Decisions:**
- **Scope/Spec Size:** Keep all five batches together in one spec (~2,300–4,500 tokens, above the 1,600 guideline) with one focused parallel subagent per batch (coordinating `docs/development-route-catalog.md` without concurrent write conflicts).
- **DW-681 (Canonical Package-Boundary Alias Policy):** Keep `ValidateCanonicalBoundaryPackageCohort` strict exact 3-part canonical SemVer (`5.3.0..5.6.0`, no `.0` fourth-revision or `+metadata` aliases), aligning with `Directory.Build.props`, `HexalithMcpCliVersion != '3.0.0'`, and submodule `Hexalith.Builds` `ValidateHexalithEventStoreReferences`; update diagnostic/comments in `Directory.Build.targets` and `docs/development-route-catalog.md` and add explicit alias-refusal guard tests while retaining alias normalization in `ValidateCatalogSdkSelection`/`ValidatePublishedCatalogSdk`.
- **DW-704 (Live Corpus & Token Bounds):** Enforce `MaxCorpusPages = 10`, `MaxCorpusTenants = 100`, and `MaxEncodedTokenLength = 8_192` in `AuthenticatedRouteState.CreateCorpusReadTokenAsync`.
- **DW-702 (Failure-Path Diagnostic Bounds):** Wrap `captureFocus`, `captureReadiness`, and the inner-catch `auditReturnFocusState` (`validate-authenticated-live-routes.mjs:380-401`) with `captureFailureDiagnostic` (`5_000 ms` per diagnostic).
