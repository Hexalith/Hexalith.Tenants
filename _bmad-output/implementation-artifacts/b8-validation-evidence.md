Existing specs settle refusal as `command_identity_conflict`, zero publication and retained evidence; there is no new public quarantine status or invented guarantee.

DW-499 stages immutable exact terminal advisory intent and independent discovery ownership atomically with terminal idempotency, pipeline/drain cleanup and publication accounting. The intent preserves original MessageId, scope, correlation, normalized causation, type, count, original range, timestamp and exact status. Repair writes only that committed record; acknowledged status writes permit actor-state intent removal. Failed/ambiguous acknowledgements use existing discard/inspection seams. Matching duplicates preserve cached outcomes and may repair; conflicting identities and unrelated owners cannot repair. Completed/domain-Rejected and successful drain are supported; PublishFailed stays recoverable and TimedOut can be ambiguous. No proof comes from aggregate head.

DW-502 verifies checkpoint command identity, actor partition, count and range, then every persisted envelope's identity, scope, contiguous sequence, correlation and causation before any resume/drain publication or evidence-release transition. Stable event MessageIds must be unique within the range. Direct resume, stale handoff, activation and reminders share refusal. Malformed/missing/legacy unverifiable evidence stays retained without domain execution, event persistence, publication, retry-budget consumption or exhaustion cleanup. A recovery owner missing its command evidence cannot become a fresh command. Transient reads remain retryable and cancellations propagate. Restore exact evidence from a trusted immutable backup through separately authorized existing recovery seams, then retry the original command/reminder; every retry revalidates it.

Fair persisted activation cursors progress beyond invalid/read-failed owners while preserving bounded entry budgets. Advisory lookup checks exact indexed identity and available cached terminal outcome. Legacy rejection drains use only matching persisted rejection classification and exact identity. Actor persistence normalizes omitted causation at the actor call boundary; standalone EventPersister fallback behavior and existing data are not rewritten.
